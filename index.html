Biography
1996 · Syktyvkar, Komi Republic

Formed in Syktyvkar at the end of 1996, the band quickly stood apart on the local scene through drum machine, sharp guitars and growled vocals. After an active run of shows from 1997 to 1999, the first period came to an end in 2001.

In 2005, Roma Diamond’s solo material included songs connected to the band, and renewed work with Andrey Zavalny helped bring the project back in 2006. Samples and orchestral layers became increasingly important to the sound, while Retaliation (2008) and Reborn (2011) established the direction of the later catalogue.

Relict (2015), Revenant (2018) and REALL (2022) continued that evolution. Across these releases, Diamond has carried the vocals and lyrics, while Zavalny has driven the music, guitars, keyboards, arrangements and production. Renaissance follows in 2026.
